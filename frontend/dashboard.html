✦ CAMPUS SERVICES & GRIEVANCE REDRESSAL ✦

Report Any Campus Issue

Select an issue category below to lodge your grievance with photo proof and campus location. Dedicated administrative cells resolve problems according to institutional SLA benchmarks.

MOBILITY & ROADS

Road Issues

Report potholes, damaged pathways, speed breakers and pavement hazards across campus roads.

WATER & UTILITIES

Water Issues

Report drinking water coolers, RO filter outages, pipeline leakages, washroom supply and drainage.

ELECTRICAL CELL

Electricity & Power

Report power cuts, broken lights, ceiling fans, damaged switchboards, classroom ACs & wiring.

HYGIENE & SANITATION

Cleanliness & Hygiene

Report overflowing dustbins, untidy academic corridors, washroom sanitization and campus litter.

ESTATE & VEHICLES

Parking & Mobility

Report vehicle parking congestion, unauthorized parking, blocked gates & bicycle stands.

ACADEMIC BLOCKS

Infrastructure & Labs

Report damaged desks, chairs, lecture hall projectors, broken doors, windows and elevator faults.

IT INFRASTRUCTURE

Internet & Wi-Fi

Report campus Wi-Fi connectivity drops, hostel router speed issues, lab LAN ports & portal outages.

HOSTEL RESIDENCE

Hostel & Living

Report hostel room geysers, furniture repairs, corridor water coolers, common rooms and hygiene.

DINING & FOOD

Mess & Cafeteria

Report food hygiene concerns, dining hall seating, drinking water points and canteen cleanliness.

LIBRARY & STUDY

Library & Reading Halls

Report reading hall lighting, AC cooling, digital library terminals, book scanners and quiet zones.

SAFETY & SECURITY

Security & Safety

Report streetlights during night hours, CCTV blindspots, boundary wall hazards and emergency access.

GENERAL GRIEVANCES

Other Issues

Report sports grounds, transport buses, departmental services or any miscellaneous campus problem.

ABOUT CAMPUSCARE

One Platform For A Better Campus

CampusCare is a student-focused platform designed to make campus issue reporting simple, transparent and efficient.

Students can report problems using images and location information instead of visiting different teams personally. The system uses AI to identify issues, detect duplicate complaints and help prioritize important problems.
