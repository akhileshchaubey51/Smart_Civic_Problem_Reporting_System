ABOUT CAMPUSCARE

Building a Better, Cleaner & Smarter Campus Together

CampusCare is an AI-driven, transparent civic reporting portal engineered specifically for the KIET institution community. It removes red tape and long email chains by connecting student reports directly with department heads and electrical, civil, sanitation, and IT supervisors.

Every grievance is backed by verified photographic proof, clear SLA turnaround deadlines, and continuous timeline audit trails ensuring accountability across all campus wings.
